Theming

The site takes your colour and nothing else: the nav, the sidebar and this paragraph keep their font, radius and spacing, so picking a theme never moves the page under you. Components take the whole theme.

Until you pick a colour the theme is ambient: the palette drifts along a gradient, and every token you see here — --primary, --accent, the borders — moves with it.

Click any empty part of the page to see the ripple. Hover the two cards above for the dot swarm.

Try the controls

Buttons, inputs and chips all read the live tokens.

Contrast is fixed on every frame: the generator nudges any pairing below 4.5:1 before it reaches the page, so the drift never drops a label below AA.

Lorem ipsum stands in for the rest of a long docs page, so you can judge the texture at the edges and how much the colour should move while you read.
